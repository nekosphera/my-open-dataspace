1. Website purpose

My Open Dataspace is a demonstration and educational environment designed to showcase interoperability, governance and security patterns for federated data spaces. Its purpose is technical, educational and non-commercial.

2. Privacy scope

Reasonable safeguards are applied to protect information handled by the platform. The main goal is to enable authentication, authorization and data-asset access flows in a demonstration context.

3. Data categories

  • Registration and authentication data required for access (for example, user email).
  • Minimal operational and audit metadata for functional traceability.
  • Aggregated usage metrics to improve technical operation of the demo environment.

4. Non-commercial use

The platform and its content are provided for educational and demonstration purposes. No direct commercial exploitation is intended and personal data is not sold to third parties.

5. Retention and minimization

Data minimization principles are applied: only information strictly necessary to operate and validate the platform is retained for the required technical period.

# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being welcoming and using inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Showing empathy toward other members of the community.

Things that do not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate.
They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this code, and will explain moderation
decisions when asked.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through the
private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response
appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers must respect the privacy and
security of anyone who reports an incident.
